Review notes & limitations

Open and Close prepare their own starting pose when necessary. Replay restarts the last sequence. Drag the timeline to pause on any intermediate frame. Skip and Reduced motion select the final pose immediately. Hiding the tab pauses playback.

The geometry is consistent throughout; no capsule images, crossfades, camera movement, or asset swaps are used. The gunmetal and brass are procedural materials. This is a simpler machined interpretation of the reference artwork, not an exact reproduction of its sculpted and weathered detailing. Pressure uses a small local particle effect; it is not a fluid simulation. Real mobile GPU performance still needs device review.
