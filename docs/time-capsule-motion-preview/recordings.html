About these recordings

These are coherent pre-rendered views of the same solid model used by the inspector. Every frame is rendered at its exact timeline position and encoded for 30 fps playback; no frame interpolation or image morphing is used. Each clip includes a half-second hold before and after the ceremony. Open and Close prepare their respective starting poses. The page is silent and starts paused.

The live WebGL inspector performed poorly on this laptop's Intel UHD 600 GPU. These clips allow motion review without that rendering load; they do not prove real-time mobile performance. The model is a simpler machined interpretation of the original artwork. Its sculpted detail and weathering remain a material-quality gap. Production is unchanged.

Direct files: Opening · Closing. Use the inspector to turn off interior light and pressure and inspect the bare geometry.
