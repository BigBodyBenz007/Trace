About this preview

These pre-rendered clips use one solid 3D vault and a fixed camera. Each MP4 carries its recorded sound on the same playback clock as the picture. Nothing plays automatically. Volume and sound controls apply only to this page session.

Slow motion and frame inspection are silent. After inspection, choose Open, Close, or Replay at normal speed to hear the sound again. Reduced Motion shows the selected resting endpoint immediately and silently. Skip also goes straight to the endpoint; Stop returns to the starting pose. Leaving this page pauses playback, and returning never resumes it automatically.

The model is a machined interpretation of the artwork; its sculpted detail and weathering are still limited. These clips demonstrate pre-rendered playback, not live mobile rendering performance. Production remains unchanged.

Direct clips: Opening with sound · Closing with sound. Source recordings and edit provenance are listed in the recording credits.
